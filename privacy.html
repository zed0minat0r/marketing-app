Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Sidekick is a product operated by Pennsylvania Technology Solutions LLC ("Penn Tech Solutions," "we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect your email, phone number, and a record of your SMS consent (what you agreed to and when). Once you use Sidekick, we collect your business name and details you tell us during setup, the content of your text messages with the service, content we generate on your behalf, photos you text in, the accounts you connect (Facebook and Instagram via Meta, Google Business Profile via Google), post performance data from those platforms, and standard network metadata (IP address, device user-agent). We do not collect payment information today; if billing launches, this policy will be updated first.

How we use it

Your phone number is used only for the message types you have opted into, and only those: account notifications (your place in line, when your early-access spot opens, account and billing status, and your results summary) and customer care replies (our replies to messages you send in - support answers, and any content you ask Sidekick to draft for you). We do not send marketing or promotional text messages, and we do not text you about anything you have not opted into. Your business info and message content are used to generate, schedule, and publish marketing content on the platforms you connect. We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not share your data with advertisers.

Subprocessors we use

To deliver the service we share specific data with the following categories of providers, each acting under our instructions: an SMS carrier (sending and receiving texts), a managed database provider (storing your account, with connected-account tokens encrypted), a generative AI provider (content generation), the platform APIs you connect (Facebook/Instagram via Meta, Google Business Profile via Google), and standard hosting infrastructure. We require each provider to handle your data only to run Sidekick and to comply with applicable privacy law.

Connected platforms

When you connect a social or business platform account, you authorize us via OAuth to publish content, read engagement metrics, and (for Google Business Profile) read and reply to reviews on your behalf. We never receive your platform passwords. You can disconnect any platform at any time by texting DISCONNECT followed by the platform name. Once disconnected, we no longer access that account and we delete the connection token within 24 hours.

Data retention

While your account is active we retain your account data, message history, and generated content. If you cancel, we delete your business data, generated content, and connected-platform tokens within 30 days. Your phone number is removed from all marketing lists immediately on cancellation. Tax, billing, and abuse-prevention records are retained as required by law.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account at any time. California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Utah residents have additional rights under their state privacy laws including the right to opt out of any sale (we do not sell) and the right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights. EU/UK residents have rights under GDPR including data portability and the right to object to processing.

Security

We use TLS in transit and encryption at rest for stored credentials. OAuth tokens for connected platforms are stored encrypted. We never see or store your platform passwords. We follow industry-standard practices but no online service is 100% secure.

Contact

Privacy questions, data requests, or to delete your account: text PRIVACY to your Sidekick number, or email us directly. Data controller: Pennsylvania Technology Solutions LLC. We respond within 7 business days.
